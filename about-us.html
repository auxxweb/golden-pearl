Welcome

Welcome to Golden Pearl

Established in the year 2016, Golden Pearl have been serving the needs of retail, commercial and Residential construction sectors.
Our ethos is excellence in design and delivery; working across architecture, master planning, interior design, and graphic design.
Our mission is to understand our clients’ cultural, functional, operational and commercial needs, drawing on our experience
to facilitate solutions that work. We strive to create the best solutions for client’s projects and to ensure high quality
and timely project execution. We believe that quality and timing project implementation
are very critical to the success of our business

Our Mission

To provide Quality
Every aspect of a project is given the same focus and attention to detail no matter how small, our clients know their brands are in safe hands.
To provide Service
To maintain the highest standards of manufacturing through precision and quality control processes, we use the latest automated machinery, thus reducing labour costs.
To provide Reliability
We can meet your requirement and timeline for one-off prototype, custom or large volume production run joinery.

Our Vision

Be the premier engineering, construction, and project management organization by achieving extraordinary results for our customers, building satisfying careers for our people, and earning a fair return on the value we deliver.
Golden Pearl is well placed to offer speedy execution in respect of Civil and Interior decoration with a large team of workforce of Civil, Interior and MEP Workers. The company has also created and strengthened in house capabilities for supervision and execution of large turnkey jobs involving the entire scope of interior works like Partition, Paneling, False Ceiling, Furniture and all Interior related Civil works, MEP and finishing works.
